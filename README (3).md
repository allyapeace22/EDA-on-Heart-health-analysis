#  Heart Health Analysis Dashboard

##  Overview

The Heart Health Analysis Dashboard is a Python-based interactive analytics project that explores heart disease risk factors using data visualization and statistical analysis. The project provides insights into patient demographics, health metrics, and disease prevalence to support healthcare decision-making.

---

##  Objectives

- Analyze patient demographics and health conditions
- Identify major heart disease risk factors
- Explore relationships between age, cholesterol, blood pressure, and heart disease
- Compare disease prevalence across different patient groups
- Generate actionable healthcare insights through visual analytics

---

##  Features

- Interactive dashboard
- KPI Summary Cards
- Age Distribution Analysis
- Heart Disease by Gender
- Chest Pain Type Analysis
- Cholesterol vs Heart Disease
- Heart Disease by Age Group
- Risk Factor Impact Summary
- Key Healthcare Insights

##  Dataset

The dataset contains patient health records, including:

- Age
- Gender
- Chest Pain Type
- Cholesterol Level
- Resting Blood Pressure
- Fasting Blood Sugar
- Exercise-Induced Angina
- Resting ECG
- Heart Disease Status

---

##  Key Insights

- Over half of the patients in the dataset have heart disease.
- Male patients show a higher prevalence of heart disease than female patients.
- Patients aged 51–55 years have the highest disease occurrence.
- Cholesterol levels above 240 mg/dL are strongly associated with heart disease.
- High cholesterol and elevated ST depression are among the strongest risk indicators.
